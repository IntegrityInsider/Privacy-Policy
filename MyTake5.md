## 1. WHO WE ARE

MyTake5 is a digital workplace health and safety pre-task risk assessment application operated by Integrity Insider (integrityinsider@gmail.com).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect information when you use the MyTake5 mobile application.

---

## 2. WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information you enter into the app

When completing a Take 5 risk assessment, you may enter:

- Your full name
- Your work order number
- Task name
- Hazard and control information
- Your handwritten signature

This information is included in the Take 5 PDF and JSON submission that is emailed directly to your nominated workplace supervisor. **Integrity Insider does not retain, store or access this submission data.**

### 2.2 Work area photographs (AI scan feature)

If you choose to use the optional AI work area scan:

- You take or select a photograph of your work area
- The photograph is transmitted securely to Anthropic PBC (USA) for AI processing
- The photograph is **immediately deleted** from your device after processing
- The photograph is **not saved** by Integrity Insider
- The photograph is **not included** in your Take 5 submission
- The AI observation text is displayed to you only and is not submitted or stored

### 2.3 Service limitation acknowledgement

Each time you start a Take 5, the date and time of your acknowledgement of the service limitation notice is recorded in your submission. This is a timestamp only — no other personal data is recorded for this purpose.

### 2.4 Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect:

- Location data
- Contact lists
- Health or biometric data (beyond your handwritten signature)
- Financial information
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Browser history or usage analytics beyond what is described above

---

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Information entered in the app is used solely to:

- Generate your Take 5 PDF and JSON submission
- Email that submission to your nominated workplace supervisor
- Record your acknowledgement of service limitations for legal compliance

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, or any purpose beyond providing the Take 5 service.

---

## 4. WHO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION WITH

### 4.1 Your workplace supervisor

Your completed Take 5 submission (PDF and JSON) is emailed directly to the supervisor email address associated with your company code. Integrity Insider does not control what your employer does with this information after receipt.

### 4.2 Anthropic PBC (USA) — AI scan only

If you use the optional AI scan feature, your work area photograph is transmitted to Anthropic PBC, located in the United States, for AI processing. This is a cross-border disclosure under Australian Privacy Principle 8 (APP 8) of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Anthropic processes the image under their privacy policy, available at privacy.anthropic.com. Anthropic does not use your image to train AI models.

### 4.3 Cloudflare Inc (USA) — infrastructure

Our app uses Cloudflare Workers (USA) to securely route AI scan requests. Cloudflare does not have access to the content of your submissions.

### 4.4 No other sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third party.

---

## 5. CROSS-BORDER DISCLOSURE

As noted above, work area photographs processed through the AI scan feature are transmitted to Anthropic PBC in the United States. This disclosure is made under APP 8 of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). By using the AI scan feature and accepting the in-app image collection notice, you consent to this cross-border disclosure.

All other submission data remains within Australia (transmitted by email to your supervisor).

---

## 6. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- The Anthropic API key is stored securely on Cloudflare's encrypted secret store — it is not accessible from the app
- Work area photographs are deleted from your device immediately after AI processing
- Integrity Insider does not maintain a database of Take 5 submissions
- Submissions are emailed directly to your supervisor and are not stored on any Integrity Insider server

---

## 7. DATA RETENTION

| Data type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Take 5 submission (PDF/JSON) | Not retained by Integrity Insider — delivered to supervisor by email only |
| Work area photograph | Deleted from device immediately after AI processing |
| AI observation text | Displayed in-app only — not stored or submitted |
| Service limitation acknowledgement timestamp | Included in submission PDF/JSON only |

## 8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

- Request access to personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate personal information
- Make a complaint about how we handle your personal information

Because Integrity Insider does not retain submission data, there is generally no personal information held by us beyond what is described in this policy.

To make a privacy request or complaint, contact us at integrityinsider@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.
